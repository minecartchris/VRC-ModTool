for the PC you play VRChat on

  1. 1. Download it and run it on your gaming PC.
  2. 2. It shows a link. Open that link in this browser.
  3. 3. That's it — it starts reporting as you.

Your key is handed over machine to machine when you open the link, so it never appears on screen for somebody to photograph, and never has to be pasted into a chat.
